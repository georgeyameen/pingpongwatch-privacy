PingPongWatch Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Hey there, Ping Pong Player!

I'm George Yameen, the creator of PingPongWatch. I built this app because I love table tennis and wanted a better way to track my game. Here's the straight talk on how your data is handled.

What PingPongWatch Does & Doesn't Do

PingPongWatch uses your Apple Watch sensors to track your ping pong shots and provide feedback. Your detailed shot data stays on your device. Only a short game summary is sent, see Analytics below.

Your Data Stays Put

PingPongWatch processes your shot tracking locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your shot stats, match scores, performance metrics, and session history stay on your devices. The one exception is the short game summary sent to Google Analytics, described below.

Health & Motion Data

To track your workouts, PingPongWatch reads motion-sensor data and, with your permission, heart rate and calorie data from Apple Health. This information is used on your device to show your session insights and to save your workouts back to the Apple Health app. Your health and motion data is never collected, sold, or sent to us or any third party.

Subscriptions

PingPongWatch offers an optional subscription. Purchases are handled securely by Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat). We never see or store your payment card details.

Analytics We Use

We use Google Analytics (by Firebase) to see how the app is used, like which screens get opened and how many games get played. When you finish a game, the app sends a short summary with the score, game length, shot counts and top speed. It never sends your name, your email, your heart rate, your calories or anything from Apple Health. Google also sees basic device info (phone model, iOS version) and a rough, city level location from your IP address. None of this is linked to you or used for ads.

Kids Can Play Too

PingPongWatch is safe for players of all ages. We don't collect any personal information that identifies users.
